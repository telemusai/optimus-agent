- Fixed native OCC-3 source captures with large contexts failing with "Provider stream stalled: no events for 120000ms". An armed capture turn buffers its whole response on the server and sends no events until it finishes. The client stream watchdog now widens its event gap and stream deadline for that attempt to the capture lease's own deadline plus a 30 s grace, clamped to the watchdog maxima. Larger operator settings are kept, a disabled limit stays disabled, and the process environment is not changed. Before this fix, only the benchmark examples raised the limits, so session handoffs always used the 120 s default.
